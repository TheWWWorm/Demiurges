The Synthets draw on the black ether: Synthesis in Etherlords, the ether of the Machine in Etherlords II. For how they play, see [The four races](races); for the other races, see [Races and peoples](story-peoples).

## Nature and magic

The Synthets replace most of their bodies with machinery, keeping a minimum of living matter, which gives them great endurance. The Etherlords II campaign calls them a fusion of mortal and machine. Their implants are grown over generations, and a hero's own will holds them together: "Synthesis is not a gift, it's a burden."

Their magic dissolves the barriers between living and dead matter, reality and illusion, time and information. Their creatures are pseudo-machines: mechanical worms, hoppers, Veloses on wheels, mechozauruses and pitiless mechanical cutters.

## Society and beliefs

- **Designations.** Heroes bear model codes rather than names: a Greek letter, a number and a nickname, such as Alpha-100F "Steel" or Beta-600F "Hammer".
- **Perfection.** Their goal is the Path to Perfection, in Etherlords II the **Great Synthesis**: cruel and bold experiments joining flesh and mechanism, in which everything rational is allowed. They speak of production targets and upgrades.
- **The Lord's commandments.** Heroes quote their Lord as law: Synthesis guards those who serve it; accept treachery, then get rid of the traitor.
- **The Cluster.** In Etherlords II a networked intelligence broadcasts orders and breaks codes, and coordinators run the cities. In the first game a Chief Oracle analysed the information spread in the ether, and concluded that the White Lord might be only a fluctuation of information.
- **Words for others.** All other races are barbarians to them; the Kinets are "Pneumatics", and the Chaots valuable experimental material. Everyone else finds the Synthets sinister and calls them "Rusties".

## In the story

- **Etherlords.** The Lord of Synthesis proposes the alliance of Iron and Fury to the Chaots. The Synthets ask their allies to capture the Kinet heroine Diamanda for their rituals, and their scientists turn her into a Synthet warrior. They defeat the Fire Konung for an element they need, and at the gates of the Temple simply decide to go on alone. See [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).
- **Etherlords II.** The **Pale Rust** consumes the Synthets' northern lands and then their capital. The soldier Alpha frees the hero Beta from Diamanda's trap, uses the Vital traitor Flon to break into a Vital fortress, destroys the pale monster that drains Axis City and faces Diamanda on the northern peninsula. Their scientists learn pale magic of their own. See [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

## Homeland and places

The Land of Synthesis lies in the north. Its capital, **Axis City**, is lost to the Pale Rust in Etherlords II, and its Shrine of Synthesis shields it from hostile magic. The Synthets rest at ether nodes, which the Rust cannot touch. See [Places](places).

Synthet engineering survives in old buildings: a Mech Worms facility built in ancient times, a Disturbance Generator once used for magic experiments, and the Purify Station, a Synthet crystal that turns their Purify spell into a weapon against the Pale.

## Resources and rituals

In Etherlords the Synthets' mineral is the **Smoke Diamond**, dug in diamond mines; their Smoke Ritual produces it. Their global spells include Biohazard and Channel Warp, and their ultimate spell is Terminal Discharge. Their castle is the Castle of Synthesis.
