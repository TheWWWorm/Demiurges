**First Strike** is scenario 3 of a series of six scenarios of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that were never released. The texts of the series (`MT_S01` to `MT_S06`) are filled in only in the English edition, which also has each briefing as the text of a spoken one; in the Russian edition the same texts are empty. No map of either edition uses them.

## Briefing

The Vitals and the Chaots are at war, and scouts have found a new way to a precious artifact in the middle of the region. Whoever reaches it first has the advantage, and the old fortifications along the way can help either side.

| Condition | Text |
|---|---|
| Objective | Destroy enemy Castle |
| Victory | Destroy enemy Castle |
| Defeat | Lose your Castle |

## What is known

Only the texts remain; no heroes, decks or program rules are tied to this map.
