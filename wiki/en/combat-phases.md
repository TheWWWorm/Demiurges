A turn belongs to one hero, the attacker. It has two phases: the attacker's **Attack/Cast Phase**, then the opponent's **Block Phase**. Then the heroes swap roles and the next turn begins. Use the **End of Phase** button, **Ctrl+E** in Etherlords, or **Tab** in Etherlords II to finish your phase. **Space** skips the current presentation.

## Start of the turn

At the start of the attacker's turn, in this order:

1. [Ether disturbance](combat) damage, once it has started.
2. Healing from the **Regeneration** skill.
3. The attacker's creatures recover (stand up) and stop being newly summoned, except under spells that keep them down.
4. The attacker draws a spell.
5. Ether channels grow and ether is refilled to the number of channels.
6. Enchantments that act at the start of the turn take effect.

## Attack/Cast Phase

The attacker casts spells and chooses which ready creatures attack. Creatures that rest, were just summoned (without **Haste**) or cannot attack stay back. **Berserk** creatures must attack. When the attacker ends the phase, the chosen creatures attack. If no creature attacks, the Block Phase is skipped.

## Block Phase

Now the defender acts: they assign blockers (and may cast the few spells allowed in this phase) to the attacking creatures. Each ready creature can block one attacker, and several creatures can block the same attacker. When the defender ends the phase, combat damage is dealt: see [Attack and defence](attack-defence).

## End of the turn

1. The attacker discards down to 5 spells in hand.
2. Damage on all creatures heals.
3. Enchantments that act at the end of the turn take effect.

## Who begins

In a [duel](duel), a coin decides who attacks first in every round. In campaign battles the map decides, as in the original games.

> In the Attack/Cast Phase you can cast any spell you can pay for. In the Block Phase only a few spells are allowed, as in the original games: Polymorph, Fog and Energize All, and two more in Etherlords II. Each card that allows it says so in its description.
