This page describes the world in which both games are set, as their manuals, briefings and dialogues present it. The plots are on [The story of Etherlords](story-el1) and [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2); the races on [Races and peoples](story-peoples).

## Ether

Everything in the world, living or not, is made of **ether**, which the manual also calls simply magic. **White ether** boils at the centre of the universe. It is the strongest of all, but unstable, and it breaks down into four lesser, coloured ethers:

| Colour | Etherlords | Etherlords II |
|---|---|---|
| red | Chaos | ether of Fire |
| blue | Motion | ether of Air |
| green | Vitality | ether of Nature |
| black | Synthesis | ether of Technology |

Each of the four intelligent races is bound to one colour, and so are the creatures that serve it. Some simple creatures have magic of their own but cannot grow beyond what their kind can do. Heroes draw white ether only in battle, through ether channels (see [How a battle works](combat)).

## Lords and heroes

Each race is ruled by its **Lord** (the English manual also says Etherlord). Lords do not fight in person. They summon **heroes**, who build castles, tame creatures, dig mines, open portals and study magic. Heroes never fight with weapons, only with sorceries, enchantments and summoned creatures.

## The White Lord and the Temple of Time

At the edge of the world stands the **Temple of Time**, the seat of the **White Lord**, master of white ether and, in Etherlords II, the "keeper of balance" of the world. Legend says the first Lord to enter the Temple becomes the new White Lord, and the old one is banished to a distant, hostile world.

## The Time of Changes

Roughly once in a thousand years the heavenly bodies line up with the centre of the universe (the Etherlords II texts speak of four suns) and the coloured ethers flow back together into white. The world is remade: mountains fall, rivers dry up, new lands rise and even magic changes. Barriers that kept the races apart fail, and the road to the Temple of Time opens, giving every Lord a chance at the White Lord's throne.

The flood of ether has side effects. Wild animals grow enraged, the dead rise, and ghostly beings form where flows of ether cross; these are the monsters that guard mines and treasures on the [adventure map](adventure-map). Etherlords II adds that the altars found on the map have appeared since the last Time of Change.

## Ancient history

The first game's briefings mention a primeval **Time of Beginnings**, from which the Vitals' Book of Life dates, and the **Wheel of Time**, which a key from the Lost City could stop and turn the other way. Long ago the Synthets attacked the Vital capital, Chebokiana; the Vitals answered with a forbidden ritual that split the mountains and buried the city, and it became the Lost City.

## The Pale and the Outer Plane

Etherlords II adds a fifth force. The **Pale** (also called colourless) creatures come from the **Outer Plane**, another world beyond a portal that the White Lord opened. They drain ether and colour from everything they touch: a hero trapped among them weakens day by day, and pale land saps a hero's experience. Each race has its own name for the disease: the Kinets call it the Pale Plague, the Synthets the Pale Rust. Pale spells fit any spellbook (see [The four races](races)).
