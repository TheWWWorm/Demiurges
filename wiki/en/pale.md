The **Pale** (also called colourless) are the new force of Etherlords II. In the story they are invaders from another world; in play they are a fifth, colourless group of creatures and spells that any hero may use. See [The world of ether](story-world) for the background.

## Where they come from

The Pale come from the **Outer Plane**, a world beyond an ancient sealed portal. The White Lord sacrificed the souls of mighty heroes to open it on the northern peninsula, and only his will keeps it open. He meant the Pale to drain the flows of ether so that the races would become docile pale shadows and no new Lord could ever take his throne. See [The White Lord](white-lord) and [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

On their own the Pale are mindless creatures without a will. Diamanda can command them, and late in the story Synthet scientists learn to summon them too.

## The plague

Wherever the Pale spread, colour and ether drain from the world. Each race names the disease in its own way:

| Race | Name | What they believe |
|---|---|---|
| Kinets | the Pale Plague | the Synthets carry it |
| Synthets | the Pale Rust | their neighbours spread it; one scientist thinks it punishes them for loving upgrades more than their Lord's commandments |
| Vitals | the pale disease, pale beasts | Synthet machines that turned on their makers |

A hero trapped among them loses strength day by day until the hero becomes colourless, feeble and meek; the Synthet hero Beta felt it as lost synchronisation and failing implants. Powerful pale monsters act as funnels through which ether is sucked out of the world. The Synthets found that their ether nodes resist the Rust.

## Pale lands

On some Etherlords II maps whole regions turn grey. A hero walking in this **pale land** loses experience, which can even lower the hero's level; the in-game text says lost experience is recovered at ether sources. The scripts widen or shrink the pale zone from chapter to chapter as the story goes on.

## Pale and bright

Pale creatures exist in two **aspects**: pale, when they are not really in the world, and bright, when they have manifested. Each family pays its own price to manifest:

| Family | Creatures | Price to manifest |
|---|---|---|
| Warriors | [Pale Soldier](card-smfx), [Pale Zealot](card-smtg), [Pale Knight](card-smwb) | exile a creature from a graveyard |
| Reapers | [Pale Scourge](card-oud1), [Pale Reaper](card-oud2), [Pale Slayer](card-oud3) | sacrifice an ether channel |
| Weavers | [Pale Spelleater](card-oue1), [Pale Spellweaver](card-oue2), [Pale Spellshifter](card-oue3) | destroy one of your enchantments |
| Mages | [Pale Channeler](card-ous1), [Pale Invoker](card-ous2), [Pale Healer](card-ous3) | sacrifice a creature |
| Punishers | [Pale Avenger](card-osj1), [Pale Revenant](card-osj2), [Pale Redeemer](card-osj3) | sacrifice a spell from your hand |

The Reapers kill weaker bright creatures and grow from them. The Punishers lie in wait as enchantments and become creatures when the enemy casts a sorcery. The boss that drains Axis City is a Pale Slayer.

## Pale spells

Pale sorceries and enchantments play on the two aspects: [Paleness](card-prwt) makes every creature enter pale, [Lock Aspect](card-prbn) and [Seal](card-plsl) stop aspect changes, [Dissipate](card-bnsh) and [Vaporize](card-gbns) send pale creatures away, and [Ripple](card-orpl), [Flux](card-oflx) and [Blast](card-obls) strike them. [Pale Warp](card-plwp) lasts only while a pale creature is in play. The Synthets' [Purify](card-prfy), strengthened by the Purify Station, destroys pale creatures outright.

The White Lord's last guardian is a colourless [Konung](card-ekon), the same creature the Synthets summon with a rod.
