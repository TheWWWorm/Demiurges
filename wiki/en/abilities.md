Abilities are shown as icons on a creature; hold the pointer over an icon for its name. The table lists the most common ones under their names in the games. Numbers in brackets vary by creature.

| Ability | What it does |
|---|---|
| Flying | Can be blocked only by flying creatures. |
| Cannot attack | Walls: they defend but never attack. |
| First Strike | Hits before its opponent, in attack and in defence. |
| Regeneration (1–3) | When destroyed, can come back by paying the given cost. |
| Trample | Damage left after destroying its blockers goes to the enemy hero. |
| Lifetap | Damage to the enemy hero heals its owner. |
| Restless | Does not rest after attacking. |
| Disenchant | Removes the enchantments of creatures it hits. |
| Creature cannot get up | Does not recover by itself after resting. |
| Berserk | Must attack whenever it can. |
| Ether Defense | Cannot be the target of spells or abilities. |
| Unblockable | Cannot be blocked. |
| Haste | Can act on the turn it is summoned. |
| Elusive | Can be blocked only by walls. |
| Team attacker | Attacks only together with a creature of its race. |
| Poisonous | Destroys a creature it damages in combat. |
| Discard (1–3) | When it damages the enemy hero, the opponent discards that many random spells. |
| Pale ability, Pale aspect | Etherlords II: the creature can pass into its pale aspect, where it neither attacks nor is attacked. |
| Spell jamming | Etherlords II: hinders the opponent's spells. |

Many creatures also have abilities you use by resting them or paying ether; their own descriptions explain them in the game. See [Attack and defence](attack-defence) for how the combat abilities work together.
