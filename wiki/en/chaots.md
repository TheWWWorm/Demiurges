The Chaots draw on the red ether: Chaos in Etherlords, the ether of Flame in Etherlords II. For how they play, see [The four races](races); for the other races, see [Races and peoples](story-peoples).

## Nature and magic

Chaots have scarlet faces like rough-hewn stone and strong, sturdy bodies. They are unruly and almost fearless, and they sweep aside whatever stands in their way. The Etherlords II manual says some call them the mightiest race; they are not, the manual adds, only the most direct: they strike first and are always at war.

Their elements are fire, lightning, lava and earthquakes. Their creatures come in aggressive packs: rats, kobolds, orcs, bats and wolves, with ogres, the [Cyclops](card-cclp) and walls of flame behind them. They have no dragons; every dragon in the game serves the Kinets.

## Society and beliefs

- **Free warriors.** Chiefs lead war-bands and meet in a great council at Jingling Rock. In Etherlords II the race is split between the **West** and the **East**, whose leaders settle disputes with duels of monsters in the Main Arena.
- **Fire.** They swear by the Flame of Chaos and the Fire of Earth. Shamans read the past and future in flames, and the **Great Shaman** guards the Chaos Eye, the race's holiest altar. The dead are given to the sacred flame.
- **Law.** Traitors are judged by the Law of Red Mountains. The briefings also mention the Red Star, by whose turns around the Chaot Tower of Sacraments they count time.
- **Customs.** Plunder and honour, songs of last stands, feasts, arena tournaments and beer. Their names sound Norse: Hellvard, Sofnir, Odmar Red-Fist, Alveig.
- **Words for others.** The Kinets are "Airheads", the Synthets "Rusties", the Vitals "green slime". To everyone else the Chaots are barbarians and fire-worshippers.

## In the story

- **Etherlords.** The Chaots open the Flaming Iron campaign with the Fire Ordeal, an ancient rite proving that their warrior spirit survived the long peace. Allied with the Synthets, they fight their old enemies the Kinets, capture Diamanda for their allies and reach the Lost City, where they meet a Synthet monster. Calling it treachery, they turn on the Synthets at the gates of the Temple of Time. See [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).
- **Etherlords II.** The young warrior Konnar of Fire Valley fights a Kinet invasion that crossed a lake on bridges of ice, sees the Great Shaman killed at the Chaos Eye and frees the City of Thundering Stones. The campaign ends in a peace with the Kinets, who pay with the Northern Island. See [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

## Homeland and places

The Chaots' homeland is a land of red mountains and volcanoes; the Synthets call them spawn of the fiery deserts. In Etherlords II it is the **Land of Chaos**, with Fire Valley, the City of Thundering Stones, its Western and Eastern Castles and Main Arena, the Island Fortress and the Chaos Eye in a volcano crater. See [Places](places).

Some old buildings on the Etherlords II map carry Chaot magic: an active volcano, an Altar of Storms that doubles lightning spells, and a Lightning Generator that the briefing says an ancient Chaot hero built.

## Resources and rituals

In Etherlords the Chaots' mineral is the **Bloody Ruby**, dug in ruby mines; their Blood Ritual produces it. Their global spells include Meteor and Chain Lightning, and their ultimate spell is the Clock of the Apocalypse. In Etherlords II all minerals became one resource, stones. The Chaot castle is the Castle of Chaos.
