**Intrusion** is a campaign of [Etherlords II](cut-content-el2) that exists only as a draft in the English edition's texts. It reuses the text keys of the first game's Vitals campaign, `MT_G`, `MT_G0` and `MT_G1`, which Etherlords II no longer needs, and fills them with working notes instead of finished texts. In the Russian edition the same keys are empty, and no map, campaign or script uses them.

## The plan of the opening

The campaign's description is not a story but a note for the team: before the campaign starts, the player was to read two illustrated stories. The draft lists their scenes:

- **The Legend of Delusion**: a colourless avatar of the White Lord heals the Vitals' sick Lake of Life and mocks them; doubt and heresy spread among the young Vitals, and a great hero leaves to seek the Tree of Life and never returns.
- **The Vision of Colorlessness**: the Vitals blame an upheaval in the Synthets' lands on Synthet experiments, a Vital finds the Lost City and sees a hostile Colourlessness there, and the leaders call for war on the Synthets and on heresy.

The White Lord, the Pale (the Colourless), the Lost City and the Temple of Time all appear in the released games. See [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2) and [The Pale](pale).

## The two missions

| Mission | Briefing in the draft | Defeat |
|---|---|---|
| Mission 1 | The Synthets attack the frontier posts of the Vitals, and the main heroine is sent to defend a post in the delta of the Middle River, which must not fall | Lose the Hero |
| Mission 2 | A placeholder: explore the map and kill the Synthets' hero to win the mission and end the campaign | Lose the Hero |

Both missions are named only "Mission 1" and "Mission 2", and their objectives are empty. The second briefing is addressed to a tester rather than a player, so these were probably test maps for an early version of the campaign.
