**Three Lakes Valley** is a map of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that was never released. Its texts are in both editions, the Russian one calling it «Долина трех озер», but no map uses them. Its text keys are marked "D", unlike those of the campaign, multiplayer and scenario maps, so it may have been meant for the demo; this is a guess.

## Briefing

A deserted valley between three lakes has blossomed in peace since time immemorial, because the warriors of the Great Lords left it alone. When word of its beauty reaches the Lord of Chaos, his warriors come to take it, and the Vitals stand up to defend it, knowing that the Chaots would turn it into a burnt desert.

## What is known

No heroes, decks or program rules are tied to this map: only its texts remain. The briefing sets the Vitals against the Chaots, each with a castle.
