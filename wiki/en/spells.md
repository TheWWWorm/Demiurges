Everything a hero does in battle is a spell. There are three kinds:

| Kind | What it does |
|---|---|
| Creature | Summons a creature that stays until it is destroyed. See [Creatures](creatures). |
| Enchantment | Stays in play and changes a creature, a hero or the whole battlefield. |
| Sorcery | Acts once and is gone: damage, healing, destruction, extra spells and more. |

## Cost and casting

Each spell costs ether, shown on its card. Some spells have a variable cost and become stronger with the extra ether you spend. A few cost health instead. You can cast spells in your [Attack/Cast Phase](combat-phases) as long as you can pay for them and they have a target; in the Block Phase only a few spells are allowed.

## Rarity

Spells are cantrips, common, rare or unique. **Cantrips** are simple, unlimited spells; in Etherlords a spell whose runes are used up is replaced by a cantrip of the same colour. Rarer spells are more powerful and more expensive in shops and in the duel editor.

## Artifacts

Some spells are artifacts: battle items with a limited number of uses. See [Runes and artifacts](runes-artifacts).

The games' own spellbook and Encyclopedia (Etherlords II) screens describe every spell.
