**Dragon Lake** is scenario 5 of a series of six scenarios of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that were never released. The texts of the series (`MT_S01` to `MT_S06`) are filled in only in the English edition, which also has each briefing as the text of a spoken one; in the Russian edition the same texts are empty. No map of either edition uses them.

## Briefing

The Chaots and the Synthets fight for the Dragon Lake, where two old dragons guard powerful artifacts. The artifacts would help to destroy the enemy castle, and the enemy is after them too.

In the game's own wiki, the full briefing from your copy of the game appears here (the Russian edition has none):

!text el1/MT_S05_DESC

The conditions, as the English edition words them:

| Condition | Text |
|---|---|
| Objective | Destroy enemy Castle |
| Victory | Destroy enemy Castle |
| Defeat | Lose your Castle |

## What is known

Only the texts remain; no heroes, decks or program rules are tied to this map.
