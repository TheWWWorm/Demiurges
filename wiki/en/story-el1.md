> This page describes the whole plot of both Etherlords campaigns, endings included.

The story of Etherlords is told in the briefings read before each campaign chapter. The background is on [The world of ether](story-world) and the races on [Races and peoples](story-peoples).

Names follow the game's English texts. The few that have no English text (two chapter titles and some terms) are translated from the Russian edition and marked *(tr.)*.

## The Time of Changes

Once in thousands of years the heavenly bodies line up with the centre of the universe and white ether floods the world. Old barriers fail, mountain passes open and the road to the **Temple of Time** at the edge of the world becomes passable. Legend says that the first Lord to enter the Temple becomes the new **White Lord**, master of white ether, and the old one is banished.

The long peace ends. Each Lord seeks an ally for the race to the Temple:

- the Lord of Synthesis offers his hand to the Lord of Chaos: strength going hand in hand with perfection, the alliance of **Iron and Fury** *(tr.)*;
- the Lord of Vitality, who hates war and death, joins the Lord of Motion, the lord of harmony and order: the alliance of **Life and Wisdom** *(tr.)*.

These two alliances are the two [campaigns](campaign-el1). Each has eight chapters; within a campaign the chapters alternate between the two allied races.

## Flaming Iron: Chaots and Synthets

1. **Fire Ordeal**. The Chaots must prove that their warrior spirit has not faded in the long peace. A Fire Spirit has appeared near their castle for the first time in centuries, and legend says only the hero held in the Wooden Cage can defeat it.
2. **Fire and Ice**. With the passes open, the old feud with the Kinets flares up. The Chaots destroy the Kinet castle so it cannot strike them from behind while their best warriors march to the Temple.
3. **Unexpected Help**. The Synthets take over: Vitals and Kinets counter-attack a Chaot border castle, and the Synthets come to defend their allies, whom they privately value as experimental material.
4. **Capture**. The Synthets ask a favour: their rituals need one of the strongest Kinets. The Chaots hunt down **Diamanda the Magnificent, Mistress of Dragons**, and must wear her down without killing her.
5. **Fire Konung**. Next on the Synthets' path to perfection is the Fire Konung, a mysterious being of flesh near the Wild Wasteland whose strength is bound to four Altars of War.
6. **Lost City**. The way to the Lost City, the ancient capital of the Vitals, is open. A legendary artifact, the **Key** to the Temple, survived there among horrors born of an ancient forbidden ritual. Beside the ruins the Chaots meet a monster, the Walking Horror.
7. **Temple of Time**. The alliance breaks, and the player chooses a side:
   - *Chaots*: the monster in the Lost City was a Synthet creature, surely part of a treacherous plan. The Synthets refuse to explain, and the Chaots punish them under the Law of Red Mountains.
   - *Synthets*: the alliance has become a burden and the Chaot castle happens to stand on the road. The Synthets' Chief Oracle, who suspects the White Lord is only a fluctuation of information, sends heroes to check.
8. **Last Battle**. The chosen race's envoy reaches the Temple gates. Only a Lord may take the final step, so the Lord fights the White Lord in person, twice, to prove himself worthy of the throne.

## Flying Leaf: Vitals and Kinets

1. **Mechanical Threat** *(tr.)*. The Vitals' Book of Life speaks of the Temple and of a key that turns the Wheel of Time. A hideous Synthet machine now roams the Vital forests, and only the hero in the Wooden Cage can defeat it.
2. **New Lands** *(tr.)*. The Chaots attack, fearsome in battle but, the Vitals say, weak of mind and overconfident. The Vitals destroy their forward castle.
3. **Help to Vitals**. The Kinets see the Chaot and Synthet alliance as a deadly danger to Stability and Order and come to their ally's aid.
4. **Aviak's Nest**. The Synthets have lured a flock of Aviaks, once subject to the Kinets, into their lands. The birds turn mad and disobedient, and the Vitals destroy the flock before the Synthets can use it.
5. **Ancient Tomb**. Kinet records place the Key in the Lost City, and the way there can only be learned at an ancient tomb in the Foggy Forest, guarded by ghostly Keepers.
6. **Lost City**. The Vitals enter their ancient capital, Chebokiana, to fetch the Key and hand it to the Kinets. Beside the Key stands a Synthet hero named **Diamanda**.
7. **Temple of Time**. The alliance breaks over Diamanda's fate:
   - *Vitals*: the Kinets, for the sake of Stability, handed their own heroine to the Synthets. The Vitals keep the Key and go to the Temple alone.
   - *Kinets*: the Vitals took the Key at the price of Diamanda. The Kinets believe the Vitals killed her without trying to save her, while the Vitals accuse the Kinets of plotting with the Synthets. The Kinets set out to establish a new order.
8. **Last Battle**. As in the other campaign, the Lord duels the White Lord at the Temple.

Each side blames the other for Diamanda's fate, but the Flaming Iron campaign shows that the Chaots captured her for the Synthets. The first game never says outright what the Synthets did with her: the Lost City map simply puts a Synthet hero, "Omega-800D Diamanda", where she stands. Etherlords II confirms it: Synthet scientists turned the Kinet heroine into a winged Synthet warrior.

## Endings

Each of the four races has its own closing film, shown after the White Lord is defeated. Following the legend, the victorious Lord takes the White Lord's place as ruler of all ether. Diamanda's story continues in [Etherlords II](story-el2).

## Other missions

The single missions are separate skirmishes with short premises: a land of crystal lakes, the legacy of an ancient Summoner, an unusual alliance of Vitals and Chaots against the Kinets, and so on. The multiplayer maps set two to four races against each other on borderlands and ore fields.
