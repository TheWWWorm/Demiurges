Creatures fight in the [phases](combat-phases) of a turn: the attacker chooses attackers in the Attack/Cast Phase, the defender chooses blockers in the Block Phase, and then damage is dealt.

## Who can attack

A creature can attack if it is ready (not resting) and was not summoned this turn, unless it has **Haste**. These cannot attack:

- creatures that **cannot attack**, such as walls;
- creatures held back by battlefield spells, for example spells that stop weak creatures;
- in Etherlords II, creatures in their **Pale aspect**.

**Berserk** creatures attack whenever they can and cannot be withdrawn. A **Team attacker** attacks only together with a creature of its own race. Attacking creatures rest afterwards unless they are **Restless**.

## Blocking

In the Block Phase every ready defending creature may block one attacker, and several creatures can block the same attacker, in the order you assign them. Limits:

- only a **Flying** creature can block a flying creature;
- an **Unblockable** creature cannot be blocked;
- an **Elusive** creature (Etherlords II) can only be blocked by walls.

## Damage

- An unblocked attacker deals its power to the defending hero.
- A blocked attacker shares its power among its blockers in order, giving each as much as it needs to be destroyed before moving on to the next.
- Every blocker strikes back with its own power.
- A creature with **First Strike** hits first; if its opponent is destroyed, the opponent does not hit back.
- A **Trample** attacker sends the damage left over after destroying its blockers on to the hero.

Damage on surviving creatures heals at the end of the turn. A hero's specialisation can add chance-based effects in combat; the hero's **Luck** and the opponent's **Suppression** change the odds.
