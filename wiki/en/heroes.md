Heroes gain experience from battles and grow through 20 levels. Each level adds health and ether income in battle.

## Skills

Each new level gives a skill point and an offer of three random skills (in Etherlords II, **Resources** is always offered first while the hero does not have it). A hero knows at most five skills, each up to rank 3.

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| Strength | +10, 20 or 30% health. |
| Regeneration | Restores health on some turns. |
| Concentration | An extra spell every few turns. |
| Channeling | Delays your ether disturbance and hastens the opponent's. |
| Learning | More experience. |
| Luck | Better chances for the hero's specialisation. |
| Suppression | Lowers the opponent's luck. |
| Artificer | More artifact uses per battle. |
| Resources | More runes per spell (Etherlords) or more charges per spell (Etherlords II). |
| Bargain | Lower shop prices (Etherlords). |

Etherlords also has skills for the map, such as **Mobility**, **Estates** and **Looting**. Its **Ethereal Attack** and **Ethereal Defense** have no effect, exactly as in the original game.

## Specialisation

Every hero has one **specialisation**: a chance-based talent in battle, such as a creature dealing double damage. Its chance grows with the battle's level. **Luck** and the opponent's **Suppression** change it.

See [Runes and artifacts](runes-artifacts) and [How a battle works](combat).
