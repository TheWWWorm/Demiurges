Autochess is a remake addition for Etherlords II. Nine heroes recruit creatures from one shared pool, set them on their half of an Etherlords battlefield and watch them fight in real time. A defeat costs health; the last hero standing wins the tournament. Open it from **Autochess** on the main menu.

## A round

1. **Preparation (45 s).** Spend ether in the shop, place creatures, then press **Start battle** (or **Ready** online).
2. **Battle (up to 90 s).** Creatures fight by themselves; **×2** speeds the battle up (online, for the whole table). A battle with ten seconds in which no one is hurt ends in a draw.
3. **Result.** The loser loses health: the round number divided by ten, plus a share for each surviving enemy creature by its price and stars. Then everyone earns ether.

Rounds 1–3 and every fifth round are **neutral rounds** against wild creatures; losing one costs health like any other defeat, counted by the wild creatures left standing. After round 3 and after every fifth round you choose one of three spells or artifacts.

## Ether

Each round brings 5 ether, 1 more for a win, interest of 1 per 10 ether saved (up to 5) and a bonus for a streak of wins or losses. **Refresh** (2 ether, key R) shows five new offers; **Lock** keeps an offer through the next refresh. **Level** (5 ether, key F) gives 5 experience; your level is the number of creatures you can field and raises the chance of costlier offers.

## Creatures and stars

Thirty-three lines of Etherlords II creatures cost 1 to 5 ether. Three copies of a line evolve into its next form: a stronger creature with more health and attack. All nine heroes buy from the same limited pool, so a line others collect becomes rare.

Creature health, attack and defense come from their original Power and Toughness. Weaknesses and armour types are gone: a hit deals attack minus defense, but never less than a fifth of the attack. Ranged creatures shoot from up to 7 metres and have less health.

## Abilities

Every original creature ability works in real time, adapted so that it is fair at every star level:

- **First strike** opens each fight with a stronger blow and delays the enemy's swing.
- **Regeneration** heals only after two seconds without taking damage.
- **Poison** of snakes deals extra damage over time instead of killing outright. **Deadly venom** of hornets and Mechanoids finishes an enemy that is still badly wounded a few seconds later, but never a three-star or 5-cost creature. Other **Poisonous** creatures poison with every blow: a third of its damage again over 3 seconds.
- **Flying**, **Trample**, **Vampire**, **Bloodthirsty**, **Unblockable** and **Breaks enchantments** keep their meaning.
- Active abilities (dragon breath, the Cyclops' strike, mantis fright, revivals and auras) recharge during battle.

## Alliances

Deploying different lines of one kind gives a bonus to all of them:

| Alliance | Lines | Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chaots | 2 / 4 / 6 | +10 / 20 / 30% attack damage |
| Kinets | 2 / 4 | faster ability recharge and attacks |
| Vitals | 2 / 4 | 1 / 2% health per second |
| Synthets | 2 / 4 | +3 / 6 defense |
| Flyers | 2 / 4 | evade 15 / 30% of attacks from the ground |
| First strike | 2 / 4 | +30 / 60% opening blow |

## Spells and artifacts

Rewards are Etherlords II enchantments and artifacts in five tiers. Each one is given to a single creature: attack and health bonuses, Lifetap, Shadow Cloak, Haste, the Rods of Fire, Lightning and Colossus, the Ring of Command and others. Click a reward, then a creature, to give it.

## Controls

- Click a reserve creature, then the ground, to deploy it; drag a creature to move it.
- Right-click a creature or offer for details. **S** sells the selected creature, **Ctrl+Z** undoes the last purchase or move.
- **W A S D** move the camera, **Q E** or right-drag rotate it, the wheel zooms, **Home** resets it. **Tab** shows all nine boards.

## Playing online

Choose **Play online** on the autochess menu. One player picks **Host** and **Start hosting** (UDP port 27190 by default; the addresses to give friends appear below); the others pick **Join** and enter the host's address. With **Online room** (the default, and the only choice in the browser) the host gets a six-letter room code instead and friends join with that code; no ports need to be opened. A player whose connection drops can join again with the same code or address and take their hero back from the computer. The seat list shows who has joined. The host presses **Start game** when everyone is in; computer heroes take the empty seats and the seat of a player who leaves. All players need the same Etherlords II installation. A single-player tournament is saved when you leave and **Continue · round N** picks it up again. **Set seed** replays the same shops and opponents.
