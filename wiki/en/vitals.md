The Vitals draw on the green ether: Vitality in Etherlords, the ether of Nature in Etherlords II. For how they play, see [The four races](races); for the other races, see [Races and peoples](story-peoples).

## Nature and magic

The Vitals are children of nature, a beautiful race that lives in green forests by murmuring rivers. They are attuned to nature at its most basic level and use the magic of animals and plants like no other race; nature herself takes their side. Their prime element is the eternal cycle of life and death.

Their allies come from forest, field and moor, "from ticks to snakes": treants, bees, mantises, finguses and the many-eyed Evil Eyes. In battle they balance attack with a strong defence.

## Society and beliefs

- **Clans.** Vitals live in clans named after plants, such as the Rowan, Blackthorn and Nut-Tree clans, under the rule of elders. Heroes call each other brother and sister and pledge "my staff is in your hands".
- **The Forest.** They call themselves the children of the Forest and invoke the Power of the Forest. A saying: the tree is strong because it grows in the forest. They swear "By Life" and greet each other with "May the Force of Life be with you".
- **Sacred places.** The Book of Life dates from the Time of Beginnings. The Spring of Life lies on an island in the Forbidden Grove, which only initiates may enter; the uninitiated are destroyed by the Forest's power. A Keeper guards the Spring day and night.
- **Words for others.** They call the Synthets soulless "Rusties" and the Chaots weak of mind; to the others they are forest barbarians.

## In the story

- **Etherlords.** The Lord of Vitality, who hates war and death, allies with the Kinets in the Flying Leaf campaign. The Vitals reach their own ancient capital, the Lost City, and take the Key to the Temple of Time, but break with the Kinets over Diamanda's fate. See [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).
- **Etherlords II.** Eileen of Rowan clan faces a Synthet invasion through mad teleporters, fights in the Forbidden Grove and saves Golden Ash-Tree City. She defeats Flon, a Vital who betrayed his people to the Synthets, and realises that the legendary Diamanda has returned. Vital legend credits the heroine Ciara with destroying Diamanda long ago. See [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

## Homeland and places

The Forest Land is wooded country of lakes and groves. Its capital in Etherlords II is **Golden Ash-Tree City**, whose city dragon Musician hears the Music of the Spheres and whose walls stand as long as an ancient treant stays. Long ago the Vital capital was **Chebokkiana**: when the Synthets attacked it, the Vitals performed a forbidden ritual that buried the city, and it became the Lost City. See [Places](places).

Old forest buildings on the map include a Hermit's Hut where a hermit blesses the forest, the Protector of Life, an ancient tree that avenges any creature killed nearby, and a Shrine of Nature that raises the forest against its defilers.

## Resources and rituals

In Etherlords the Vitals' mineral is the **Poison Emerald**, dug in emerald mines; their Snake Ritual produces it. Their global spells include Gifts of the Forest and Blessing of the Forest, and their ultimate spell, Ether Calm, stops ether combat altogether. Their castle is the Castle of Vitality in the first game and the Castle of Life in the second.
