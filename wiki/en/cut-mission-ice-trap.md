**Ice Trap** is scenario 4 of a series of six scenarios of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that were never released. The texts of the series (`MT_S01` to `MT_S06`) are filled in only in the English edition, which also has each briefing as the text of a spoken one; in the Russian edition the same texts are empty. No map of either edition uses them.

## Briefing

The Kinets have revived a lost ritual that makes the ice fields grow, and the lands of the Chaots and Synthets are already cut off by water and ice. Every race fights for the Kinets' castle, which controls the ice.

|---|---|
| Objective | Destroy enemy Castles |
| Victory | Destroy enemy Castles |
| Defeat | Lose your Castle |

## What is known

Only the texts remain; no heroes, decks or program rules are tied to this map.
