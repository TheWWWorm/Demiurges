The named places of the world of ether, as the briefings, dialogues and map labels give them. The plots are on [The story of Etherlords](story-el1) and [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

## The Temple of Time

The **Temple of Time** stands at the edge of the world. It is the seat of the White Lord and the goal of every Lord in a Time of Changes; the first to enter it is said to become the new White Lord. In the first game the road to it opens only once a rival race is beaten, and only a Lord may take the last step inside. In Etherlords II the White Lord's castle and his **Portal** to the Outer Plane stand on the northern peninsula. See [The White Lord](white-lord).

## Places of Etherlords

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| Lost City | Chebokkiana, the ancient capital of the Vitals, buried by a forbidden ritual when the Synthets attacked it. In the ruins of its town centre lies the Key to the Temple of Time |
| Foggy Forest | Home of an ancient tomb guarded by ghostly Keepers; its fog brings illusions and weak sight |
| Wild Wasteland | Lair of the Fire Konung, whose strength comes from four Altars of War |
| Vault of True Knowledge | Kinet archive with documents from the Beginning of Time |
| Sacred Forest | Vital forest, which has grown and died eight times since the Lost City fell |
| Red Mountains | Chaot homeland, which gives its name to the Chaots' law |
| Wooden Cage | The prison of trees where a legendary hero waits to be freed |
| Snowflake Maze | Ice labyrinth of the yearly Kinet tournament |
| Borderlands | Where the lands of all four races meet; the road to new lands is said to begin there |
