Demiurges follows the rules of the original games. It runs on modern systems and changes only what this page lists.

## Added

- Both games in one program, switched from the main menu, each with its own rules, saves and settings.
- Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and the browser; touch controls.
- [Remake settings](options): soft shadows, terrain detail, sharpening, anti-aliasing and more.
- The automatic camera can be interrupted by moving the camera yourself.
- This wiki, in English and Russian, in the game and on the web.

## Not available

- Network play. Local hot seat [duels](duel) work.
- Loading saves of the original games. Original Etherlords II replays can be watched.
- Intro and ending videos in the browser.

## Not yet exact

- Computer opponents follow the original's approach but are not an exact copy of its decisions.
- The darkening of a few large spells, the timing of some ability effects and Etherlords II map particles are approximations.
- The remake's own screens follow the language of your game. A few rare error messages and the battle log are still in English.

## Kept on purpose

Some behaviour of the originals looks like a mistake but is kept because the games work that way:

- The Etherlords skills **Ethereal Attack** and **Ethereal Defense** do nothing.
- Random battle events of Etherlords never happen, as in the original.
- Several Etherlords global spells only cost upkeep.
- As in the originals, computer players never answer alliance or trade offers.

See [Problems and reporting](troubleshooting) for known gaps.
