The wiki is available in two places with the same articles:

- **in the game**: the **Wiki** button on the main menu and on the game files screen, the **Wiki** button under the Etherlords II Esc menus in battle and on the map, or **F1** on any screen. The game waits while you read; **Esc**, **F1** or **Close** returns to it;
- **on the web** at [demiurges.wwworm.com/wiki](https://demiurges.wwworm.com/wiki/), free to read without game files, an account or the engine. The browser version's start page links to it.

## Languages

Every article is written in English and Russian. Switch with **EN / RU** at the top of the in-game wiki or with the language list on the website. Your choice is remembered. Until you choose, the in-game wiki follows the language of your game's texts and the website follows your browser.

Names of game things (races, phases, menus) follow the original games' own English and Russian texts where they apply.

## What it covers

The wiki explains how to play the games on this engine and how the engine differs from the originals. It does not reproduce the games' own texts, pictures or card descriptions; for the cards themselves, use the games' Encyclopedia and spellbook screens.
