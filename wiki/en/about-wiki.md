The wiki is available in two places with the same articles:

- **in the game**: the **Wiki** button on the main menu and on the game files screen, the **Wiki** button under the Etherlords II Esc menus in battle and on the map, or **F6** on any screen. The game waits while you read. **Esc**, **F6** or **Close** returns to it.
- **on the web** at [demiurges.wwworm.com/wiki](https://demiurges.wwworm.com/wiki/), free to read without game files, an account or the engine. The browser version's start page links to it.

## Languages

Every article is written in English and Russian. Switch with **EN / RU** at the top of the in-game wiki or with the language list on the website. Your choice is remembered. Until you choose, the in-game wiki follows the language of your game's texts and the website follows your browser.

Names of game things (races, phases, menus) follow the original games' own English and Russian texts where they apply.

## What it covers

The guides explain how to play the games on this engine and how the engine differs from the originals. The **encyclopedia** has a page for every creature, sorcery, enchantment, artifact, skill and specialization of both games, with its statistics, prices and description. On the website, creature pages show a picture of the creature; in the game they show the creature itself, which you can turn and zoom.

The encyclopedia in the game is built from your own copy, so it follows your game's language and data. The website's encyclopedia was exported from the original Russian and English (GOG) editions. Names, descriptions and pictures of the games' content belong to their owners.

To preview attacks, hit reactions, deaths, and other actions with their original sounds, press **F6** in the game and open a creature page. Choose an action below the 3D model and press **Play**. See [About creatures](creatures) for the full controls.
