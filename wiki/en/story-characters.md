> This page reveals the fates of the main characters.

The named characters of both games. The two central ones have pages of their own: [Diamanda](diamanda) and [The White Lord](white-lord). The plots are on [The story of Etherlords](story-el1) and [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

## The White Lord and Diamanda

- **The White Lord.** Master of white ether, who lives in the Temple of Time. In the first game the Lord of the victorious race duels him for his throne. In Etherlords II, to escape the cycle that brings a new usurper every Time of Change, he opens a portal to the Outer Plane, raises Diamanda as his tool and plans to sacrifice her. She kills him.
- **Diamanda.** Once "Diamanda the Magnificent, Mistress of Dragons", a Kinet heroine. In the first game the Chaots captured her for the Synthets, whose scientists turned her into a winged Synthet warrior; her loss broke the alliance of Vitals and Kinets. Later the Vital heroine Ciara destroyed her. In Etherlords II the White Lord brings her back as a puppet. She steals the ethers of Life and Chaos, understands her master's lie, kills him, and stays to guard the closing Portal. She is also the hero of the Etherlords II training camp.
