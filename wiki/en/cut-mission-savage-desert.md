**Savage Desert** is scenario 1 of a series of six scenarios of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that were never released. The texts of the series (`MT_S01` to `MT_S06`) are filled in only in the English edition, which also has each briefing as the text of a spoken one; in the Russian edition the same texts are empty. No map of either edition uses them.

## Briefing

The Chaots have just crushed the Synthets' elite troops, and only one enemy is left in their way: a renegade Cyclops, entrenched with a strong guard in the north-eastern mountains. The Chaots are to destroy him.

|---|---|

| Victory | Defeat Cyclops |
| Defeat | none |

## What the program still knows

This is the only scenario of the series the program itself names: it knows the map `smission01` and gives it a rule of its own. The mission is won when a human player's hero defeats the [Cyclops](card-cclp), and the usual victory, defeating every enemy, does not count on it. Nothing else of the map is left.
