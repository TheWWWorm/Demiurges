The four races as the games' texts describe them, in brief. Each has a page of its own: [Chaots](chaots), [Kinets](kinets), [Vitals](vitals) and [Synthets](synthets); the invaders of Etherlords II are on [The Pale](pale). For how each plays, see [The four races](races); for the world they share, see [The world of ether](story-world).

## Chaots

**Red ether.** Faces like rough-hewn stone, strong and sturdy bodies, almost no fear: whatever stands in the way is to be swept aside. Their elements are fire, lightning, lava and earthquakes; their creatures are aggressive packs of rats, wolves, Orcs and Kobolds.

- **Society.** "Chaots are free warriors." Chiefs lead war-bands and meet in council at Jingling Rock. In Etherlords II the race is split between the West and the East, whose leaders settle disputes with duels of monsters in the arena.
- **Faith.** They swear by the Flame of Chaos and the Fire of the Earth. Shamans read the past and future in flames; the Great Shaman guards the Chaos Eye, a holy altar in a volcano crater. The dead are given to the sacred flame, and traitors are judged by the Law of Red Mountains.
- **Customs.** Plunder and glory, songs of last stands, feasts, tournaments and beer. Their names sound Norse: Hellvard, Sofnir, Odmar Red-Fist.
- **Others.** They call the Kinets "Airheads", the Synthets "Rusties" and the Vitals "green slime". Others call them barbarians and fire-worshippers.

## Kinets

**Blue ether.** The most intellectual race: impassive faces, slender frames, long lives. They value life, but knowledge more ("Strength through knowledge"). Their allies are creatures that seek freedom and knowledge: Aviaks, Lamias, elementals and the mighty dragons. Their elements are water, wind, snowstorms and floods.

- **Society.** In Etherlords II they are ruled by the **Council of Sixteen**, whose members are called the Betters; its head is addressed as Blessed One. Their capital, the City of the Eight Gates, is a thousand years old.
- **Ideals.** Stability and Order, the Harmony of the Spheres, and the Era of Enlightened Harmony. The first game mentions the Vault of True Knowledge and a yearly tournament in an ice labyrinth, whose winner may speak with the Greatest Teacher.
- **Customs.** Bows, formal invitations, polite forms of address and fine calligraphy. Their mages raise bridges of ice.
- **Others.** The Synthets call them "Pneumatics"; they call the Synthets "rusty scum" and the Chaots barbarians.

## Vitals

**Green ether.** Children of nature, who live in forests by rivers and command animal and plant magic like no other race. Their allies are creatures of forest, field and moor: snakes, ticks, bees and mantises. Their element is the eternal cycle of life and death.

- **Society.** Clans named after plants (the Rowan, Blackthorn and Nut-Tree clans) under elders. Their capital in Etherlords II is Golden Ash-Tree City; their ancient capital, Chebokiana, became the Lost City.
- **Faith.** The Book of Life from the Time of Beginnings, the Sacred Forest, and the Spring of Life in the Forbidden Grove, which the uninitiated may not enter. They greet each other with "May the Force of Life be with you" and swear "By Life".
- **Customs.** Heroes call each other brother and sister. A saying: the tree is strong because it grows in the forest.
- **Others.** They call the Synthets soulless "Rusties"; to others they are forest barbarians.

## Synthets

**Black ether.** They replace most of their bodies with machinery, keeping a minimum of living flesh, and command mechanical creatures from mechanical worms to merciless cutters. Their magic dissolves the line between living and dead matter, reality and illusion, time and information.

- **Society.** Heroes bear model designations rather than names: a Greek letter, a number and a nickname, such as Alpha-100F "Steel". Implants are grown over generations, and a hero's will holds them together. In Etherlords II a networked intelligence, the **Cluster**, broadcasts orders, and coordinators run the cities; the capital is Axis City.
- **Goal.** The Path to Perfection, later the Great Synthesis: endless experiments joining flesh and mechanism, in which everything rational is allowed. Their Lord's commandments are quoted as law. In the first game their Chief Oracle doubted that the White Lord existed at all.
- **Others.** All other races are barbarians to them, and the Chaots are also valuable experimental material. Everyone else finds them sinister.

## Alliances and enmities

In the first game's Time of Changes the races paired off: Chaots with Synthets (Iron and Fury) and Vitals with Kinets (Life and Wisdom). Both alliances broke at the gates of the Temple. Chaots and Kinets are old enemies, as are Vitals and Synthets. In the single missions any pairing is possible. See [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).

## Other creatures

Neutral creatures roam everywhere: mountain Orcs, wild ogres, Kobolds, Cyclopes, dragons, Treants, crazed bees and nests of ticks. Hermits such as Rulana sell spells, and old buildings recall ancient heroes. In Etherlords II the **Pale** creatures come from beyond the world; they have no will of their own and drain the ether of everything around them.
