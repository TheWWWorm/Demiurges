> This page reveals the White Lord's fate.

The **White Lord** is the master of white ether, the source of all other ethers, and the ruler of the world of ether. He lives in the Temple of Time at the edge of the world. The first game calls him the Lord of all Lords and the master of all living creatures in the world of ether; Etherlords II, the keeper of balance. See [The world of ether](story-world).

## The legend

Legend says the first Lord to enter the Temple of Time becomes the new White Lord, and the old one is banished to a distant, hostile world. This is why every Time of Changes, when the road to the Temple opens, turns the races against each other.

Not everyone believes in him. In the first game the Synthets' Chief Oracle concluded that the White Lord was most likely an erroneous fluctuation of information energy, or a hallucination, and sent heroes to check.

## In Etherlords

At the end of each campaign the Lord of the victorious race reaches the Temple gates. Only a Lord may take the last step, so the Lord fights the White Lord in person, in two battles, to prove worthy of the throne. In the game he is a hero of level 15 and then 20, with the specialization Dragon Skin. See [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).

## In Etherlords II

Countless days have passed since he took his throne, and he fears the next Time of Change and the usurper it will bring. To end the cycle he means to drain the ether flows and make the races docile pale shadows, calling it bringing calm and establishing order.

He lured mighty heroes to the Abandoned City and devoured their souls to open a **Portal** to the Outer Plane, letting in the [Pale](pale). He raised **Diamanda** from the dead as his puppet and sent her to steal the ethers of the races, giving her fifty days and threatening to abandon her as a soulless shadow if she failed. His true aim was to sacrifice her, once she held all four ethers, to keep the Portal open forever.

In the Kinet capital a plotter used his name as cover, claiming the White Lord demanded the blood of the Head of the Sixteen. A Kinet black dragon in Synthet lands spoke of a coming Master heard in the music of the Spheres.

## His end

Diamanda saw through him. At his castle by the Portal she offered him a warrior's choice: give up his reign, or leave for the pale world and let the Portal close behind him. He answered that it did not matter whether she came obedient or rebellious. She defeated his guardian, a colourless [Konung](card-ekon), and killed him. His enchantments broke, and the Portal began to close. See [Diamanda](diamanda) and [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).
