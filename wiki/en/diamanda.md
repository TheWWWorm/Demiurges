> This page reveals the whole of Diamanda's story.

**Diamanda** is the one character who runs through both games. She begins as a Kinet heroine, becomes a Synthet warrior, dies, returns as the White Lord's puppet and ends as his killer. Etherlords II calls her "the ancient creation of heroes' magic".

## In Etherlords

In the first game she is **Diamanda the Magnificent, Lady of Dragons**, a Kinet heroine of great power: every dragon of the world serves the Kinets. During the Time of Changes she ventures into Synthet lands, where the Chaots, at their Synthet allies' request, capture her alive for the Synthets' rituals.

The Synthet scientists turn her into a Synthet warrior, something possible only in a Time of Change. In the Lost City the Vitals find her standing beside the Key to the Temple as the Synthet hero "Omega-800D Diamanda". Her loss breaks the alliance of Vitals and Kinets: each blames the other, and neither knows the truth. See [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).

## The terrible Diamanda

Etherlords II remembers her as a legend. Synthet and Kinet natures were joined in her; she wore armour and had huge wings, and she terrorised the Vitals until the Vital heroine **Ciara** destroyed her. The Synthet hero Beta still recognises her from deep memory as "daughter of Air".

## The White Lord's puppet

At the edge of the world, where the souls of dead heroes rise, the White Lord found her soul: two currents of ether, black and blue, still fighting each other. He healed her body, found in a northern crypt, and woke her without a will of her own. Because she already held two ethers, he ordered her to master the remaining two.

In the four race campaigns of Etherlords II she is the mysterious armoured stranger: she traps a Kinet or Synthet hero, kills the Keeper of the Spring of Life and the Great Shaman of the Chaots, and waits on the northern peninsula, where Mirabell or Alpha defeats "Diamanda". See [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

## Awakening

Her own campaign tells the other side. With each deed her memories return: the bright clothes of heroes, a blinding flash, a long sleep. She marvels at the life of the Vital forest and the fire of the Chaots and asks her master whether he really means to destroy them.

Back at the Portal she understands that he lied: he is mad with the desire for immortality. Having merged all four ethers, she has become, in his words, the greatest artifact in the universe, and her sacrifice is to keep the Portal open forever. She defeats his guardian, kills the White Lord, and stays to guard the Portal until it closes, letting the heroes of the world believe they won alone.

## In play

In Etherlords she is a level 8 Kinet hero with the specialization of casting spells for free; as Omega-800D she is a level 12 Synthet. In Etherlords II she is the hero of the training camp and of the last campaign, and her portrait changes as she absorbs the ethers of Life and Chaos. See also [The White Lord](white-lord) and [Characters](story-characters).
