The original controls work as in the games. These keys are the most useful.

## Keyboard and mouse

| Key | Battle | Map |
|---|---|---|
| Left click | choose a spell, creature or target | move, talk, visit or attack |
| Space | skip the current presentation | continue a conversation |
| Ctrl+E (EL1) / Tab (EL2) | end the current phase | — |
| Esc | cancel targeting, or open the game menu | close a window, or open the game menu |
| F1 | open this wiki | open this wiki |
| F5 / F9 | quick save / quick load | quick save / quick load |
| Right drag, wheel | turn and zoom the camera | turn and zoom the camera |
| R | reset the camera | — |
| W A S D, arrows | — | move the camera |
| H or Home | — | follow the selected hero |
| I / K | — | spellbook / skills (first game) |
| N | — | end the turn (first game) or the day (second game) |

## Touch screens

Phones, tablets and touch browsers have their own controls:

| Gesture | Battle | Map |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | as a left click | as a left click |
| Touch and hold | right click; on a button, its tooltip | right click (Etherlords II: the information panel while held) |
| Drag with one finger | turn the camera | move the map |
| Drag or twist with two fingers | turn and tilt the camera | turn and tilt the camera |
| Pinch | zoom | zoom |

A small bar at the top of the screen gives **Back** (Esc) everywhere, **Camera** (reset) in battle and **Save** / **Load** on the map. On Android the Back button also works as Esc.

## In the wiki

**Alt+Left** and **Alt+Right** go back and forward, **Ctrl+F** searches, and **Esc** or **F1** closes the wiki. The game waits while the wiki is open.

On a [creature page](creatures), choose an action under the 3D model and press **Play** to preview it with its original sound when available.

## Console

Press the backtick key under Esc or **F10** to open the console. Use **Help** to list the current commands, **Tab** to complete one, and **Up** or **Down** to browse history. In a local game, select **Enable commands** or enter `EtherRevelation`.

Type `help` followed by a space and press **Tab** to browse topics available on the current screen. Press **Enter** to read the selected topic.

On the adventure map, `player` lists the commanders. `view resources` shows your wallet. Use `view resources 0` for the neutral player, `view resources 1` for the first commander, and so on. `give Root 5` adds five roots in Etherlords I, and `give Herbs 5` adds five herbs in Etherlords II. Use `help give` to see the resource names for your edition.

In Etherlords I, `open_tomb` reveals the Ancient Tomb on the campaign map that contains it.

In battle, `view creature` lists both armies and their creature IDs. Use `view creature ID` for state and damage, or `view creature stats ID`, `view creature basestats ID`, and `view creature modifiers ID` to inspect stats and enchantments. `add enemy CODE` summons a creature for the opposing hero. Gameplay commands require a local game.

Use `view spells` to look up spell codes. Add a color, a spell class, or both to narrow the list. For example, `view spells blue enchantment` lists blue enchantments. Press **Tab** with the cursor in a filter to complete it.
