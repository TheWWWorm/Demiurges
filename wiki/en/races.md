Etherlords has four races, each drawing on its own colour of ether. A race's spells share a style of play.

| Race | Ether | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Chaots | red Ether of Flame | direct damage and aggressive creatures |
| Vitals | green Ether of Nature | strong creatures, growth and healing |
| Kinets | blue Ether of Air | flyers, tricks and control over the opponent's spells |
| Synthets | black Ether of the Machine | destruction, weakening and draining the opponent |

Etherlords II adds **pale** (colourless) spells and Diamanda, the ancient creation of heroes' magic, the subject of its last [campaign](campaign-el2). Pale spells fit any spellbook.

## Races and spellbooks

In a [duel](duel), a hero's spellbook must match the hero's race (pale spells are always allowed in Etherlords II). In the campaigns a hero can collect spells of any colour.

See [Spells](spells) for the kinds of spells each race uses.
