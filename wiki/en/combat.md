Every battle in Etherlords is a duel between two heroes, the Etherlords. Each hero casts spells from a spellbook: creatures fight, enchantments change the battle and sorceries act at once. The first hero whose health falls to 0 loses. Demiurges follows the original rules of each game; the same structure applies to both games, with differences in the numbers.

## Health

A hero's health grows with the hero's level. The **Strength** skill adds 10, 20 or 30%. In campaign battles the hero starts with the health they have on the map. If both heroes reach 0 at the same moment, the battle is a draw.

## Ether and ether channels

Spells cost **ether**. Each hero has **ether channels**, which start at 0 and grow by 0 to 2 at the start of every turn, faster for higher-level heroes. Each turn the hero's ether is refilled to the number of channels, so ether you do not spend is lost. Some Etherlords II spells also need a minimum number of channels, and spells with a variable cost take as much extra ether as you choose.

**Ether disturbance** stops a battle from dragging on: after a number of turns that grows with the hero's level, the hero starts losing health every turn. In Etherlords the damage grows by 1 each turn; in Etherlords II it grows much faster. The **Channeling** skill delays your own disturbance and hastens your opponent's.

## The hand and the spellbook

Both heroes start with **5 spells** in hand, dealt in turn. After that, every turn starts with one draw (the side that begins does not draw on the very first turn). Extra draws come from the **Concentration** skill and some spells. At the end of your turn you keep at most 5 spells.

Spells are drawn at random from the spellbook, without repeats until every spell in the book has been drawn. How often a spell can be used differs between the games: see [Runes and artifacts](runes-artifacts).

## Creatures

A hero controls at most **10 creatures**. A newly summoned creature rests: it cannot attack or use abilities that need it to rest until its owner's next turn, unless it has **Haste**. Damage on creatures heals at the end of every turn; destroyed creatures go to their owner's graveyard. See [Creatures](creatures) and [Creature abilities](abilities).
