**Balance of Terror** is scenario 2 of a series of six scenarios of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that were never released. The texts of the series (`MT_S01` to `MT_S06`) are filled in only in the English edition, which also has each briefing as the text of a spoken one; in the Russian edition the same texts are empty. No map of either edition uses them.

## Briefing

The peace ends when new passes through the mountains open the way into the lands of the Chaots. The scouts find no Chaots there, and the Synthets, Vitals and Kinets begin to fight over the empty land.

## What is known

Only the texts remain; no heroes, decks or program rules are tied to this map.
