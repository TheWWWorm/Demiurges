**Synthets Menace** is scenario 6 of a series of six scenarios of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that were never released. The texts of the series (`MT_S01` to `MT_S06`) are filled in only in the English edition, which also has each briefing as the text of a spoken one; in the Russian edition the same texts are empty. No map of either edition uses them.

## Briefing

The briefing opens with the Latin motto *si vis pacem, para bellum*. The Synthets invade the lands of the Vitals once more. The Vitals cannot hold out against their attack head-on, but the Gates stand in the Synthets' way and give the Vitals the chance to strike back first.

|---|---|
| Objective | Destroy enemy Castle |
| Victory | Destroy enemy Castle |
| Defeat | Lose your Castle |

## What is known

Only the texts remain; no heroes, decks or program rules are tied to this map.
