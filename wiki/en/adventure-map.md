Between battles your heroes travel the adventure map: they visit buildings, collect resources and meet enemies. Touching an enemy hero or creature starts a [battle](combat).

## Etherlords

- Each player has a **castle**. Its energy is the player's life: a ruined castle is out of the game. Heroes next to an enemy castle damage it every night.
- Orders are given during the day and carried out at night. **N** ends the turn.
- **Resources**: Mandrake Root, Black Lotus, Bloody Ruby, Poison Emerald, Star Sapphire, Smoke Diamond and Frozen Flames, from their glades, mines and glaciers. **Ether nodes** give ether every turn; ether pays for **global spells** and the upkeep of heroes, and unused ether repairs the castle.
- **Buildings**: Rune Shops, Spell Shops and creature shops of three levels that can be captured; Teachers; springs such as the Spring of Life; merchant wagons; prisons; altars; Forts; and more.
- Five difficulty levels: Easy, Normal, Hard, Expert and Impossible.

## Etherlords II

- The game runs by days; a hero can travel a limited distance each day. **N** ends the day.
- **Resources**: Plants, Stones and Frozen Flames.
- **Buildings**: spell shops, Sorcerer's Labs, Beacons, power nodes, specialisation shrines, Prisons, rune barriers and Forts.
- Crossing the **Pale lands** costs heroes experience.
- The bag screen holds the hero's 16 spells, at most 4 copies of one.
- Three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Hard.
