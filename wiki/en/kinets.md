The Kinets draw on the blue ether: Motion in Etherlords, the ether of Air in Etherlords II. For how they play, see [The four races](races); for the other races, see [Races and peoples](story-peoples).

## Nature and magic

The Kinets are the most intellectual race, the "winged philosopher race" of Etherlords II. They have impassive faces and slender, delicate frames, and they live long lives. They value life, but knowledge more: "Strength through knowledge." A Kinet hero prefers speed to brute force, parrying and striking back until the enemy is exhausted.

Their elements are water and wind, snowstorms and floods. Their allies are creatures that seek freedom and knowledge: Aviaks, Lamias, Gibberlings, halos, spirits, all four elementals and all four dragons, the mightiest creatures of the world.

## Society and beliefs

- **Order.** Their ideals are Stability and Order and the Harmony of the Spheres, which they also shout as a battle cry. In Etherlords II their castle is the Castle of Ordering, their priests serve the Temple of Order, and their age is the Era of Enlightened Harmony.
- **The Council of Sixteen.** In Etherlords II the Kinets are ruled by a council whose members are called the Betters. Its head, addressed as Blessed One, was Dominicius. Other offices include the Keeper of Keys.
- **Knowledge.** The first game mentions the Vault of True Knowledge, with documents from the Beginning of Time, and a yearly tournament in an ice labyrinth whose winner may speak with the Greatest Teacher.
- **Customs.** Bows, formal invitations, "noble" and "honourable" forms of address, and fine calligraphy. The seer Droncan speaks in verse.
- **Words for others.** They call the Synthets rusty scum and the Chaots barbarians; the Synthets call them "Pneumatics" and the Chaots "Airheads".

## In the story

- **Etherlords.** Allied with the Vitals in the Flying Leaf campaign, the Kinets find the way to the Lost City through an ancient tomb. Their heroine **Diamanda**, Mistress of Dragons, is captured by the Chaots for the Synthets. Accused by the Vitals of giving her up, they turn on their ally at the gates of the Temple. See [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).
- **Etherlords II.** The Pale Plague eats Kinet lands and drives them to invade the Chaots. The young messenger Mirabell frees the noble Rinalda from Diamanda's trap, uncovers a plot against the Head of the Sixteen, performs a rite that weakens the plague, and faces Diamanda on the northern peninsula. See [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).

## Homeland and places

The Lands of Movement lie by the sea, with islands, canyons and ice. Their capital, the **City of Eight Gates**, is a thousand years old. Other places include the Market Place, Fish Island, Snake Canyon, the Temple of Order and the Shrines of Order, which shield the Kinet lands from hostile magic. The **northern peninsula**, where the White Lord's portal opens, lies on their border with the Synthets. See [Places](places).

Old Kinet buildings on the map include a Temple of Frost Wind that sends ice storms, an Aviak shrine that drives Aviaks into a frenzy, and an Ancestral Tomb where the souls of fallen warriors return to help.

## Resources and rituals

In Etherlords the Kinets' mineral is the **Star Sapphire**, dug in sapphire mines; their Star Ritual produces it. Their global spells include Shield of Ice and Hypnotism, and their ultimate spell is Ether Leak. Their castle in the first game is the Castle of Motion.
