> This page describes the whole plot of Etherlords II, endings included.

Etherlords II tells one story five times. The four race campaigns start at the same moment and show the same events through different eyes, often on the same maps; the last campaign, Diamanda's, reveals what was behind them. The [campaign order](campaign-el2) is: Vitals or Chaots first, then Synthets or Kinets, then Diamanda. The background is on [The world of ether](story-world), the people on [Characters](story-characters).

## Before the game

A long peace has passed since the events of [Etherlords](story-el1), and a new Time of Change is near. The **White Lord** fears that it will bring a new Lord to take his throne, as it has before. To end the cycle for good he means to drain the flows of ether and turn the races into docile pale shadows.

He sensed another world, the **Outer Plane**, behind an ancient sealed portal. With ghostly visions he lured mighty heroes of all four races to the **Abandoned City**, where his whirlwind devoured their souls; the shock opened a **Portal** on the bleak northern peninsula, and only his will keeps it open. Through it come the **Pale** creatures, which suck ether and colour out of the world.

At the edge of the world, among the rising souls of dead heroes, he found the soul of **Diamanda**, the Kinet heroine whom the Synthets once turned into a warrior of their own and whom the Vital heroine Ciara destroyed. Its black and blue currents were still fighting each other. He healed her body, found in a northern crypt, and woke her as his puppet: she is to absorb all four ethers for a ritual that will keep the Portal open forever.

## Vitals: Eileen of Rowan clan

The young heroine **Eileen** is sent north by the Vital elders.

1. **Synthet Incursion.** There has been no news from the Blackthorn Fortress. Its commander Tienan reports a Synthet invasion: on its first day the ether flows twisted, the teleporters went mad and Synthets began to come out of them. Eileen relieves the outpost that Kilan the Fearless held alone for eight days. The hermit Rulana has a vision of an armoured woman, "like a Synthet but with clear eyes", walking south.
2. **The Spring of Life.** Synthets enter the Forbidden Grove, where only initiates may go. Eileen enters anyway and meets a strange "scout" who completes a ritual at the Spring and escapes.
3. **Golden Ash-Tree City.** The Vital capital is besieged by all three other races, and the first pale creatures appear: hollow shells that do not smell of machines. Eileen frees the city's dragon Musician, the ancient Treant who keeps the walls standing, and Elder Vian's son Narmes.
4. **Traitor!** In a Synthet-held frontier fortress Eileen defeats Flon, a Vital who sold himself to the Synthets, and his Walking Horror. Vian compares her to the great Ciara, who once destroyed the terrible Diamanda, and Eileen realises that the "scout" at the Spring matched Diamanda's description.

The Vital campaign ends with the mystery unsolved.

## Chaots: Konnar of Fire Valley

**Konnar** is a young warrior after loot and glory.

1. **The Kinet Threat.** The Kinets, who never invade, have crossed a lake on bridges of ice and killed the chief Hellvard. With a hammer hardened in the Flame of Chaos, Konnar breaks the bridges.
2. **The Chaos Eye.** At the holy altar in a volcano crater pilgrims are vanishing. Konnar glimpses a half-Synthet, half-Kinet warrior from the old tales, but arrives too late: the Great Shaman is already dead.
3. **City of Thundering Stones.** The city fell to a night raid while the West and the East quarrelled. Alveig, Lightning of the West, and Krogerd, Hammer of the East, settle who leads the war with a duel of monsters: her Fighting Dragon against his Cyclops. Konnar frees the dragon and the city.
4. **The Island Fortress.** Konnar breaks through to the Kinet peninsula. The Kinet leaders ask for peace, saying a great misfortune drove them to war. Alveig accepts and takes the Northern Island as the price.

## Kinets: Mirabell

**Mirabell** is a young, very formal messenger with a love of calligraphy.

1. **More than a Messenger.** Sent to invite the noble Rinalda to the Council of Sixteen, which has met over the **Pale Plague**, Mirabell finds her trapped on an island and slowly drained by the plague. A warrior much like Diamanda broke the bridge behind her. Mirabell rebuilds it and frees Rinalda in time.
2. **A Fortress Siege.** The plague is eating Kinet lands, and the Council orders the Big Campaign to take new land from the Chaots: the same siege of the Island Fortress that Konnar fights from the other side.
3. **The Council of Sixteen.** The plague reaches the capital. Mirabell finds that the Keeper of Keys lured the Head of the Sixteen into a cage, claiming that the White Lord demanded his blood, to take his seat. Priests of the Temple of Order teach her a rite that unites the souls of all four races and a pale monster, and it weakens the plague.
4. **Confrontation.** The seer Droncan foretells that whoever defeats Diamanda on the northern peninsula will end the plague. There the Synthet hero Alpha admits Diamanda is too strong for him, and Mirabell defeats her.

## Synthets: Alpha-100F "Steel"

**Alpha** is a disciplined Synthet soldier.

1. **Discovery.** Alpha frees the hero Beta from a dungeon where the **Pale Rust** is draining him. Beta recognises his captor from deep memory: Diamanda, "daughter of Air", long dead.
2. **Assault on the Fortress.** With their northern lands lost to the Rust, the Synthets seek a foothold in the south. The Vital traitor Flon shows them a secret passage, and Alpha turns a teleporter to bring Walking Horrors into a Vital fortress.
3. **Save Axis City.** The Rust swallows the Synthet capital. The records of a scientist who turned himself into a measuring device and went mad reveal that a powerful pale monster is a funnel through which ether leaves the world. Alpha destroys it, and the Rust recedes from the city.
4. **Trail's End.** The strongest funnel is on the northern peninsula, where Diamanda waits. Mirabell, beaten by her, leaves the fight to Alpha, who defeats Diamanda. The scientists find only a fragment of a teleporter.

The Kinet and Synthet finales are the same battle from two sides, and in each the player's hero strikes the last blow. Diamanda's campaign suggests that neither race really ended the plague.

## Diamanda

The last campaign, called the story of the White Lord, opens with his monologue (see *Before the game*).

1. **Awakening.** To wake her soul, Diamanda must destroy a former "brother". She chooses a teleporter: to the Kinet lands, where she traps Rinalda on her island, or to the Synthet lands, where she traps Beta. If she is too slow, Mirabell or Alpha frees the prisoner and the White Lord abandons her. Afterwards fragments of memory return: bright clothes of heroes, a blinding flash, a long sleep, and a question of whose will drives her.
2. **Theft of Vitality.** She kills the Keeper of the Spring of Life and takes the Vital ether, while the White Lord deals with Eileen. Marvelling at the living forest, she asks her master whether he really wants to destroy it.
3. **Violation of Chaos.** At the Chaos Eye she kills the Great Shaman and takes the ether of Chaos, escaping Konnar. (Chapters 2 and 3 come in either order, depending on her first choice.)
4. **The Final Awakening.** Back at the Portal, Diamanda understands that her master lied about bringing calm: he is mad with the desire for immortality. He tells her that, having merged all four ethers, she has become the greatest artifact in the universe, and her sacrifice will keep the Portal open forever. She defeats his mighty guardian and kills the White Lord.

## Ending

With the White Lord dead, his spells break. The heroes of the world of ether will cleanse their lands and believe they defeated the pale disease themselves. Diamanda decides that the Portal must vanish without a trace, and she stays on guard until it closes, sensing that her destiny is about to be fulfilled.

The game does not say whether the "Diamanda" defeated by Mirabell or Alpha on the northern peninsula, of whom only a fragment of a teleporter remained, was the guardian of the closing Portal.
