**Snowflake Maze** is a map of [Etherlords](cut-content-el1) that was never released. Its texts are in both editions, the Russian one calling it «Снежный лабиринт» ("Snow Labyrinth"), but no map uses them.

## Briefing

Once a year the wisest and mightiest of the Kinets meet in an ice maze to compete and to honour their Lord. The winner may talk with the Greatest Teacher about the boundlessness of knowledge, and such a talk is said to bring luck and success for the whole year that follows.

## Heroes

Two Kinet heroes that no map places fit the briefing: a Lamia called **Santa Claus** and an Aviak called **Father Frost**, both of level 3, with decks named "New Year 2002 Lamia" and "New Year 2002 Aviak". A Kinet tournament at the turn of the year with these two heroes was probably a holiday map for New Year 2002, a few weeks after the game came out.

## In Demiurges

Demiurges rebuilds the Snowflake Maze as a single mission of its own, listed after [Evil Dale](cut-mission-evil-dale). The [random map](adventure-map) generator lays it out from a fixed recipe on a 96-tile map of the Kinets' snow:

- Three Kinet lords take part in the tournament, each with a castle and a homeland of mines and shops round the maze: the player, and two computer rivals whose first heroes are Santa Claus and Father Frost, with their own decks. The player's first hero comes at their level, the third, with two skills to choose.
- The ice maze fills the middle of the map: a snowflake of four rings and six arms of ice, each arm running on outside the rings as a spike with two branches. Each ring opens in three places and each band between rings is closed by three arms, so every way in winds about two thirds of the way round before it reaches the heart. From each castle the walk to the heart is about as long.
- At the heart stands a Teacher, the "Greatest Teacher" of the briefing, and an Aviak Elder of the fourth level guards each of the three ways into it, the same at every door.
- The mission keeps the briefing's goal: destroy the rival castles, and keep your own.
- The hero speaks at the start, at the maze and at its heart, and has words with Santa Claus and Father Frost on meeting them and after beating them, in message boxes with their portraits. The original has no lines for the mission beyond its briefing, so these are Demiurges' own, written after the dialogues of Etherlords II.
- Both editions show their own texts.

This is a reconstruction: no map or rule of the mission survives. The tournament's sides, the maze and the Teacher at its heart are Demiurges' reading of the briefing and the two heroes.
