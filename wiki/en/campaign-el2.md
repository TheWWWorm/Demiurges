Etherlords II has five campaigns, played in a set order. Open them from **Single › Campaign**.

1. Start with the **Vitals** (the green Ether of Nature) or the **Chaots** (the red Ether of Flame).
2. The Vitals campaign leads on to the **Synthets** (the black Ether of the Machine), the Chaots campaign to the **Kinets** (the blue Ether of Air).
3. The last campaign belongs to **Diamanda**, the ancient creation of heroes' magic.

The race campaigns have four chapters each, the last one five. The main hero, money and progress carry over between chapters. Each chapter can be played at three difficulty levels.

The remake awards 100 points per chapter won, multiplied by the difficulty level (1–3). Completing a campaign adds its total to the campaign table in **Single › Mission › Hall of Fame**.

## Tutorial and missions

- **Single › Tutorial** teaches the map (adventures) and battles (combat).
- **Single › Mission** plays the single missions.

See [The adventure map](adventure-map) and [Races](races).

The plot of all five campaigns is told in [The story of Etherlords II](story-el2).
