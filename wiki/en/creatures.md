Creatures are summoned with creature [spells](spells) and fight for their hero.

## Power and toughness

Each creature has **power**, the damage it deals in combat, and **toughness**, the damage it can take. Damage on a creature heals at the end of each turn, so a creature is destroyed only when it takes as much damage as its toughness within one turn.

## Resting

A creature that attacks or uses certain abilities **rests** and stays down until its owner's next turn. A newly summoned creature also rests unless it has **Haste**. Resting creatures cannot block.

## Abilities

Most creatures have abilities, shown as icons. See [Creature abilities](abilities) for the common ones and [Attack and defence](attack-defence) for how they affect combat.

## Limits

A hero can control at most 10 creatures. Destroyed creatures go to their owner's graveyard; some spells bring them back.
