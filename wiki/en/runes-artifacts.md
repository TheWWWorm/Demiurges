## Runes (Etherlords)

In Etherlords every spell in the book has **runes**: charges that limit how often it can be cast. When the runes of a spell are used up, a drawn copy is replaced by a random cantrip of the same colour. Buy runes in Rune Shops and Sorcerer's Labs on the [map](adventure-map); the **Resources** skill raises how many a spell can hold. Cantrips need no runes.

## Spell charges (Etherlords II)

In Etherlords II every spell in the bag starts each battle with 1, 2, 4 or 7 charges, depending on the hero's **Resources** skill. The bag holds 16 spells, at most 4 copies of one spell.

## Artifacts

Both games have **artifacts**: battle items with charges. An artifact can be used from a given round of the battle and then needs a number of rounds before it can be used again. A hero can use artifacts once per battle in total, or 2, 4 or 8 times with the **Artificer** skill.

- In Etherlords, artifacts are found on the map.
- In Etherlords II, heroes keep their artifacts and their charges after battle. Blank artifacts can be made into new ones and spent artifacts can be recharged.

See [Heroes and levels](heroes) for the skills mentioned here.
