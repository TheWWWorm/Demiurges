The browser version runs at [demiurges.wwworm.com](https://demiurges.wwworm.com/). There is nothing to install: open the page, press **Play** and give it your game files.

## Giving it your game files

Choose one of:

- the GOG offline installer (`setup_etherlords*.exe`). It is not run: the browser unpacks the game files from it once and checks each one. This takes a few minutes and needs about 1.5 GB (Etherlords) or 3 GB (Etherlords II) of browser storage;
- the folder of your installed game, in desktop browsers;
- a [data pack](installing) (`.elpack`).

Repeat this for the other game to keep both. Your files are **never uploaded**: the game reads them on your own device and keeps its copy in the browser's storage, so the next start is quick.

## Requirements

- WebGL 2 and a desktop-class browser, or a recent phone with plenty of memory. Unpacking a large game needs a lot of memory for a short time; if it fails on a phone, use a data pack.
- Intro and ending videos are not played in the browser yet.
- Browsers can clear their storage. Keep your installer or pack, and remember that clearing the site's data also removes your saves.
