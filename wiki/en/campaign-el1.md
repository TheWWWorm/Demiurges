Etherlords has two campaigns, each played by two of the [four races](races):

| Campaign | Races |
|---|---|
| Flying Leaf | Vitals and Kinets |
| Flaming Iron | Chaots and Synthets |

Start one from **New Game › Campaign**. Each campaign has eight chapters. At the seventh you choose a branch, and the last chapter follows your choice. If you lose a chapter, you play it again.

## Score

Your score for a chapter depends on how many days you needed compared with the expected number, and on the difficulty. The best scores are kept in the **Hall of Fame**.

## Single missions

**New Game › Single Mission** plays the single missions on their own, with their own saves.

See [The adventure map](adventure-map) for what happens on the map.

The plot of both campaigns is told in [The story of Etherlords](story-el1).
